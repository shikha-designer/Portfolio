Designing systems

I'm a Product Designer and UI/UX Lead with 7+ years crafting enterprise-grade digital products for manufacturing, automotive, food & beverage and energy industries. I bridge complex industrial processes with intuitive human interfaces.

My work spans pixel-level UI craft to system-wide product strategy — from shop floors and control rooms to executive dashboards — all demanding zero-tolerance for ambiguity and error.

Specialised in vision inspection systems, traceability platforms, CMMS, EMS and operational intelligence tools. Every design decision is grounded in how real operators think, move and react under pressure.
